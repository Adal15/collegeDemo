Admission Procedure

  • Admission in bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) course will be held in the month of April or May every year, through the CET conducted by State Nodel University (L.N. Mithila University, Darbhanga). Prescribed application form and prospectus may be obtained from the office of the State Nodel University. Application Form can be downloaded from official Website of the State Nodel University and Eligible candidates can online apply the CET B. Ed. form.
