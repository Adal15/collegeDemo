Admission Procedure

  • Admission in bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) course will be held in the month of April or May every year, through the CET conducted by State Nodel University (L.N. Mithila University, Darbhanga). Prescribed application form and prospectus may be obtained from the office of the State Nodel University. Application Form can be downloaded from official Website of the State Nodel University and Eligible candidates can online apply the CET B. Ed. form.

Eligibility

  • A candidate seeking admission in B.Ed. course must have passed the Bachelor of examination in Arts, Science, Commerce or Humanities with minimum 50% marks from any recognized University of India.

How to Apply

Application for admission should be made on the prescribed form which can be obtained online from the website of the State Nodel University on payment of prescribed fee.

  • * Incomplete application form will not be accepted.

Selection Procedure

Candidate seeking admission should attach self-attested photocopy of the following documents with their application form:

  • Photocopy of certificate of Matriculation or S.S Examination showing the date of birth of the candidate.
  • Photocopy of CLC/DLC/TC.
  • Photocopy of all marks sheet.
  • Photocopy of all Certificates.
  • Photocopy of Character Certificate.
  • Passport size colour photograph.
  • Two self address envelop with well pasted stamp of Rs. 25.00 each.
  • Candidate must be qualify the CET conducted by L.N. Mithila University, Darbhanga

Documents to be submitted at the time of admission

  • Original Marks card.
  • Original CLC/DLC/TC.
  • Original Admit card.
  • Original Migration certificate (if required.)
  • Four passport size color photograph.
  • Undertaking by the candidate.
  • Copy of Aadhar Card.
  • Two self address envelop with well pasted stamp of Rs. 25.00 each.

Fee Structure

  • Selected Candidates will be required to deposit Demand Draft of Rs. 90,000 at the time of admission in first year and Rs. 60,000 in second year for full course.
  • Fee may be changed as per the direction of University or State Government.
